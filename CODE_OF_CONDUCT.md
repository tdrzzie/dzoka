# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and affected users respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing and disclosure of private recovery or identity information are unacceptable. Discuss technical disagreements with evidence and constructive language.

Maintainers may remove harmful content and restrict participation proportionately. Report conduct concerns privately through an available maintainer contact; do not publish another person's private information. A dedicated confidential reporting channel and documented appeal process are required before broader community launch.
