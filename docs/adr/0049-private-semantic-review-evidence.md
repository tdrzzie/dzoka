# 0049. Record private semantic review and adjudication evidence

- Status: Accepted scoped implementation; real reviewer/data acceptance remains separate
- Date: 2026-11-17

The mandatory source gate passes. M4's reference protocol already binds measurements to explicit inputs/profiles/references and preserves incomplete coverage. Its acceptance checklist also requires semantic review, disagreement and adjudication evidence; an unstructured document or metric alone does not implement that workflow. Add an offline, private review protocol for English drafts and grounded English attributes. Shona work remains deferred.

Use only existing reviewed standard-library/private-file helpers. No runtime dependency, inference source, model, image, profile identity or existing service/management lock changes. Operational execution requires a fresh immutable, receipt-verified management release. Review files never read live tenant databases, call providers or execute adapters.

Freeze a hash-bound English packet with exact baseline/candidate profile and projection identities, source bytes, reference/projected artifacts, declared split groups and explicit AVAILABLE/TRUNCATED/UNAVAILABLE observations. Validate every hash and declared split before summarizing judgments. Two to eight distinct pseudonymous reviewers each submit a complete case/role matrix, distinguishing assessed, unreviewed and provider-unavailable observations. Bind reviewer kind, UTC timestamp and private authority-evidence digest. Human/automated/fixture kinds and independence are declarations, not identity or qualification certification. Fixture reviewers are prohibited in reference-review scope.

Record grounding, uncertainty, privacy and safety judgments as PASS/FAIL/UNCERTAIN. Missing review and unavailable/truncated inference remain distinct coverage states. No majority vote silently resolves disagreement. Explicit adjudications bind the complete, order-independent review-set digest, a different declared reviewer, authority/rationale evidence and exact case/role/dimension. Reject stale review sets, self-adjudication, duplicate decisions and decisions on unreviewed/incomplete or unanimous evidence. An unresolved disagreement remains unresolved.

Outputs omit raw source/reference/proposal, case/slice names and reviewer identities. Retain their hashes, exact protocol source identities, counts and per-case dimension resolution. Results always leave model approval NOT_ESTABLISHED and property-release authorization NOT_GRANTED. Neither agreement nor adjudication activates fingerprints or proves factual/semantic correctness. Real accountable reviewers, permissions, qualifications and representative quality approval remain external acceptance inputs; the protocol makes their evidence reviewable without inventing them.

Keep exclusive output creation, private ACL/reparse checks, strict JSON/schema/type validation and bounded files/total bytes. Verify corruption, changed input/profile/packet, group leakage, missing/duplicate reviews, incomplete coverage, disagreement/adjudication and output privacy. Native verification must use actual isolated English inference and the protected CLI, exercising broad-read rejection and overwrite refusal. Authored review fixtures certify the workflow only.

Alternatives considered: leaving decisions in free-form documents would not enforce input/review-set identity or incomplete coverage; treating generated judgments as independent human references would misrepresent their authority; automatic majority voting would hide unresolved disagreements. The chosen offline protocol adds no provider access and preserves every earlier evidence version. It costs operators an explicit packet/review preparation step and requires separate task-specific projections and accountable acceptance. It cannot independently discover training overlap, dishonest group declarations or reviewer conflicts of interest.
