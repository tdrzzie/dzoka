Preserve **both Apache-2.0 and MIT obligations** for their respective collection/export/model contributions; neither statement removes upstream attribution/NOTICE requirements. These compatible permissive declarations are recorded explicitly rather than rewritten as an exclusive grant. Dataset/training/production legal and quality assessment is separate; no COCO/ImageNet dataset or sample photo is copied here. Complete notices before redistributing binaries.

Inspected Jina's config/projection dimension (768), mean text-pooling configuration, serialized BERT tokenizer/special tokens, processor image size/mean/std/resize policy and primary ONNX usage/output names. The pinned graph exports are loaded directly; the config's custom-code mappings are not followed or executed. The adapter declares its own half-pixel bilinear, shortest-224/center-224 image processing rather than claiming exact bicubic publisher-pipeline/benchmark equivalence. The paired shared key binds this policy, quantized graphs and a 512-token bound/explicit L2 normalization together. It is not automatically compatible with other CLIP/Jina/export/runtime versions.

Inspected SSD's uint8 NHWC input and named count/normalized-yxyx-box/score/class outputs, and FCN's RGB/ImageNet normalization plus main 21-class per-pixel logits (background plus Pascal VOC class subset). Bound SSD input to 300-square pixels and at most 20 thresholded boxes; map boxes back to source coordinates. Bound FCN input to 256-square pixels and export a 64-square semantic grid with canonical RLE. These deliberate resource profiles do not inherit published detection/segmentation accuracy or produce instance/ownership identity. No default facial recognition.
