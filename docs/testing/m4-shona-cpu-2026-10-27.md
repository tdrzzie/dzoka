## Native probe observations

The checked-in probe sources produce nine authored rows across all five profiles. The final complete run's private probe log is authoritative; observations are conformance fixtures, not accuracy rates or calibrated confidence.

Single-word GlotLID probes show why the policy is conservative: “around” scores English strongly, while “black”, “bag”, “Lenovo” and “muJoina” do not meet the word policy. This document-level classifier has no proven token-level calibration. Digit-bearing tokens can accompany accepted English words; they cannot independently establish language. Missed fragments remain explicit limitations rather than hidden rewrites. No general precision/recall gain is claimed.
