### F08 · Notification consent & delivery

**Status:** designed

- Channel-specific template editor and variable safety
- Source/target locale preview with review gating
- Explicit channel opt-in/withdrawal
- Throttled, unknown, rejected and confirmed-delivery branches

**Authority boundary:** Consent and provider operation state are separate from custody.

### F09 · Courier quote, consent & reconciliation

**Status:** designed

- Private route and expired/unserviceable quote branches
- Dedicated staff booking with current review/custody
- Separate physical courier handoff
- Cancellation confirmed/unknown/too-late branches

**Authority boundary:** Provider-vault route consent; booking does not change custody; unknown sends reconcile.

### F11 · Voluntary reward & financial dispute

**Status:** designed

- Explicit immutable terms and payment consent
- Declined, unknown, reserve and settlement states
- Independent conflict-free dispute decision
- Confirmed refund/reversal with compensating ledger and unsupported outcomes

**Authority boundary:** Payment never gates recovery; immutable terms and independently reviewed reconciliation.

### F12 · Registration, tags & finder relay

**Status:** designed

- Private QR/NFC issuance and secure-download/print layout
- NFC write-success/retry flow
- Explicit revoke/rotate confirmation and old-tag denial
- Finder privacy/phrase limits

**Authority boundary:** A tag scan neither establishes ownership nor creates release authority.

### F15 · Unclaimed property disposition

**Status:** designed

- Configurable institution policy/eligibility not invented legal defaults
- Recipient and destination evidence
- Independent approval/hold and stale-version blocks
- Final audited disposition confirmation

**Authority boundary:** Retention and legal hold checked before an audited policy-authorised terminal action.

### F16 · Scoped investigation & crop access

**Status:** designed

- Editable integer source region and disclosure mask with validation
- Changed plan requires fresh independent approval
- Unmasked metadata-only path
- Grant revocation and governed purge outcome

**Authority boundary:** Camera/time/purpose/expiry/budget grant, local footage and independent crop review.

### F19 · Organisation access & policy

**Status:** designed

- IdP enrollment handoff separate from local membership
- Site/permission matrix and independent-review conflicts
- Operator-only membership grant/revoke request and receipt
- Policy change impact and stale-version confirmation

**Authority boundary:** Least privilege, independent reviewer, versioned local policies and site scope.

### F21 · Connectors, sources & API clients

**Status:** designed

- Scoped partner client setup without printing secrets
- Origin/permission/rotation review and signed test failure
- Permitted source terms/provenance/retention form
- Channel/template localisation handoff

**Authority boundary:** Approved origins/terms, secret references, signed callbacks and scoped clients.

### F23 · Governed data, evaluation & rollout

**Status:** designed

- Frozen dataset editor, consent and source-validity blocks
- Independent source-bound adjudication form
- Ranking/coverage/calibration separate evaluation visualisation
- Independent promotion and compare-version rollback confirmation

**Authority boundary:** Versioned input/model provenance, independent labels/approval, scoped calibration and valid rollback.

### F24 · Analytics, movement & optional prevention

**Status:** designed

- All specified report families selectable with definitions/denominators
- Suppression/no-data/permission export states
- Observed vs inferred recovery paths with uncertainty
- Optional provider adoption and revocable device consent

**Authority boundary:** Tenant aggregates and uncertainty; inferred transitions do not establish possession.

### F25 · Responsive access & critical workflows

**Status:** designed

- Route-family breakpoint contract for all flows, not six isolated examples
- Mobile/tablet/desktop state navigation and focus/error behavior
- Long-text/locale and reduced-bandwidth variants
- Keyboard and screen-reader design annotations with no conformance claim

**Authority boundary:** Representative desktop, tablet and mobile adaptations; complete breakpoint testing remains separate.
